Privacy Policy

Baja Connect · Last updated August 12, 2026

Baja Connect is a community platform that connects people, animals, and resources across Baja California Sur. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it, in plain language.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign up we collect your name, your general area (e.g. "San José del Cabo"), your language preference, and your phone number or Google account email.
  • Content you post. Animal reports, photos, community posts, comments, and reactions are stored so the community can see them. Your display name appears alongside them.
  • Location you choose to share. When you place a pin on a report or tap the "my location" button, that location is used to show the right place on the map. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Feedback. Notes you send through "Help Us Improve" go privately to the Baja Connect team.

What we do with it

  • Show animal reports, rescues, fundraisers, and community posts to other users.
  • Send you the notifications you have switched on (you can turn each type off anytime in your account menu).
  • Keep the vet and rescue directory accurate.
  • Improve the product using anonymous, aggregate usage patterns.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information. To anyone, ever.
  • We do not show advertising based on your data.
  • We do not share your phone number publicly — only your display name and area appear on posts.

Services we rely on

Baja Connect runs on Google Firebase (accounts, database, photo storage) and Google Maps (the map itself). Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States, protected by security rules that only allow you to edit your own content.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete your own reports, posts, and comments at any time.
  • Turn any notification off in Account → Notification settings.
  • Delete your account and its data by writing to hola@bajaconnect.org — we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

Baja Connect is intended for people 16 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will say so clearly in the app before the change takes effect.

Questions? hola@bajaconnect.org · Baja Connect, Baja California Sur, México
