Community Guidelines

Baja Connect · Last updated August 12, 2026

Baja Connect exists so that more animals get helped, faster. Everything here follows from that. Una comunidad conectada ayuda a más animales.

Do

  • Report what you actually saw. Accurate locations and honest descriptions are what make the map trustworthy.
  • Be kind and assume good intent. Members range from lifelong locals to brand-new arrivals, in English and Spanish. Patience in both languages.
  • Respect the animals. Do not put yourself or an animal at risk for a photo. When in doubt, call one of the vets or rescue groups in the directory.
  • Credit the people doing the work. Rescuers, fosters, and clinic staff are this community's heroes.

Don't

  • No selling or breeding animals. Baja Connect is for rescue, adoption, and care — never commerce in animals.
  • No harassment, insults, or public accusations. If you believe an animal is being mistreated, report it to the appropriate group or authority rather than starting a pile-on.
  • No misinformation. Do not post fundraisers or reports you cannot stand behind.
  • No spam. Businesses in the directory are welcome; repeated self-promotion in the community feed is not.

Fundraisers

Fundraisers on Baja Connect link to the organization's own donation channel. Give directly to organizations you trust — and organizations, keep your supporters updated on how funds were used.

Enforcement

Posts that break these guidelines may be removed, and repeat or serious violations can lead to account suspension. We will always tell you why. To report a problem, use "Help Us Improve" in the account menu or write to hola@bajaconnect.org.

Together, we connect. They thrive. 🐾
